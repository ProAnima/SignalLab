# Code of conduct

Signal Lab is built by people who bring up shows, installations and networks,
test services and write software — in many languages and from many places. We
want taking part to be good for everyone.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new or who write in
  a language that is not their first.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Disagree with arguments, and accept a decision
  once it is made.
- Assume good intent; ask before you conclude.
- Give credit, and take responsibility for your mistakes.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal or political
  attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, intimidation, or encouraging any of the above.
- Using Signal Lab or this project's spaces to attack hosts and networks you are
  not authorised to test.

## Where it applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and wherever you
represent the project.

## Reporting

Write to **info@proanima.net**. Reports are read by the maintainers and treated
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions
that break this code, and may ban someone from the project, temporarily or for
good.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
